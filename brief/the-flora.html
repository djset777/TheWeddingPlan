The Brief

The Flora

Outdoor jungle garden party.

Plant shows the bloom itself. Styled shows it in an approved arrangement.

Tier 1·Non-Negotiables
Areca Palm Dypsis lutescens

Wide, pleated fronds fan out for structural scale: the bold architectural green that anchors an arch or backdrop.

Hibiscus Hibiscus rosa-sinensis

Oversized, yellow blooms provide dramatic scale and bold color. These backyard mainstays are brewed into traditional, healing Caribbean teas.

Monstera Monstera deliciosa

Deep forest green leaves with dramatic cutouts form the primary structural anchor, channeling the shaded, tangled energy of native island woodlands.

Beehive Ginger Zingiber spectabile

Waxy, layered gold cones add a vibrant, distinctive texture. Thriving in deep shade, these architectural gems mirror the lush floor of mature island forests.

Fan Palm Fronds Livistona chinensis

Wide, radiating fronds provide structural scale for arches. The Hispaniolan Taíno harvested these guano palm leaves to weave sturdy baskets and cordage.

Heliconia Heliconia psittacorum

Vibrant yellow, sculptural points shoot upward for an active, architectural layer. This native rainforest beauty continually draws in local island hummingbirds.

Banana Leaf Musa

Massive, deep green leaves offer an expansive canopy and dramatic scale. They filter the intense island sun, providing lush, essential shade across the land.

Pincushion Protea Leucospermum cordifolium 'Yellow Bird'

Spiky, warm gold pincushions add a bold sculptural element. Their sun-gold color pulls the warm Dominican light straight into the arrangement.

Green Amaranthus Amaranthus caudatus

Long, draping green strands create a dramatic, curtain-like effect. They drop heavily, mimicking the vertical flow of a secluded rainforest waterfall.

Tier 2·Strong Yes
Bird of Paradise Strelitzia reginae 'Mandela's Gold'

Striking, bright yellow blooms offer a rare, sculpted look. Only the golden 'Mandela's Gold' cultivar belongs here, never the standard orange and blue.

Sword Fern Nephrolepis exaltata

Arching, forest green fronds provide dense background volume. They evoke the cool, sheltered depth of native limestone caverns and ravines.

Leucadendron Leucadendron orientale

Layered, golden-yellow cones offer a distinct supporting texture. Their radiant hue anchors the display, reflecting the sunlit tones of the coast.

Celosia Celosia argentea

Feathery, golden-yellow plumes offer a deeply textured, gestural layer. Their fluid forms mirror the dancing crests of native tropical songbirds.

Ti Plant Cordyline fruticosa

Broad, glossy green blades add upright structure at mid-height. A staple of Caribbean gardens, thriving in the island heat.

Anthurium Anthurium andraeanum 'Midori'

Glossy, heart-shaped blooms in green and golden-yellow add sculptural texture. The thick, lacquered petals hold their shape for hours in heat.

Tier 3·Florist's Discretion
Gloriosa Lily Gloriosa superba

Distinctive, flame-like golden blooms add a rare vertical texture. Their climbing habit mimics the wild, reaching vines of native tropical canopies.

Spanish Moss Tillandsia usneoides

Trailing silver fibers introduce an airy, organic texture. Draped overhead, it softens hard structures into something that feels grown rather than built.

Mokara Orchid Mokara 'Sunshine Bright Yellow'

Bright yellow blooms cluster along tall, cascading stems. Long-lasting and vivid, they add a tropical, sculptural accent to the arrangement.

Plumeria Plumeria rubra

Fragrant, velvety white and yellow blooms offer a soft, lush texture. They carry a deep history as traditional adornments across native island rituals.
