The Brief

The Flora

Outdoor jungle garden party.

Tier 1 · Non-Negotiables
Areca Palm Dypsis lutescens Wide, pleated fronds in deep yellow-green. The bold architectural green that anchors arches and backdrops.
Hibiscus Hibiscus rosa-sinensis Oversized, sunshine-yellow blooms. Dramatic scale and bold color for focal accents in bouquets and table florals.
Monstera Monstera deliciosa Deep forest green leaves with dramatic cutouts. The primary structural anchor of any arrangement.
Heliconia Heliconia psittacorum Vibrant yellow, sculptural points shooting upward. An active, architectural layer.
Fan Palm Fronds Livistona chinensis Wide, pleated, radiating fronds. Structural scale for arches and overhead installations.
Beehive Ginger Zingiber spectabile Waxy, layered gold cones. A vibrant, distinctive texture that reads as gold from across the room.
Banana Leaf Musa Massive, deep green leaves. Expansive canopy and dramatic scale — backdrop and installation only.
Pincushion Protea Leucospermum cordifolium 'Yellow Bird' Spiky, warm gold pincushions. A bold sculptural element that pulls warm light into the arrangement.
Tier 2 · Strong Yes
Green Amaranthus Amaranthus caudatus Long, draping green strands. A dramatic, curtain-like effect for verticals and edges.
Bird of Paradise Strelitzia reginae 'Mandela's Gold' Striking, bright yellow blooms. Only 'Mandela's Gold' — never the standard orange and blue.
Sword Fern Nephrolepis exaltata Arching, forest green fronds. Dense background volume for depth.
Leucadendron Leucadendron orientale Layered, golden-yellow cones. A distinct supporting texture that anchors the display.
Celosia Celosia argentea — gold/yellow, feathered plume Feathery, golden-yellow plumes. A deeply textured, gestural layer.
Ti Plant Cordyline fruticosa, green Broad, glossy green blades. Upright structure at mid-height, a staple of Caribbean gardens.
Calla Lily Zantedeschia 'Yellow Mammoth' Furled golden trumpets on bare, arching stems. A clean vertical line that adds height without bulk.
Anthurium Anthurium andraeanum 'Midori' Glossy, heart-shaped blooms in green and golden-yellow. The lacquered petals hold their shape for hours in heat.
Tier 3 · Florist's Discretion
Gloriosa Lily Gloriosa superba Distinctive, flame-like golden blooms. A rare vertical texture, used sparingly.
Spanish Moss Tillandsia usneoides Trailing silver fibers. Softens hard structures into something that feels grown rather than built.
Mokara Orchid Mokara 'Sunshine Bright Yellow' Bright yellow blooms clustered along tall, cascading stems. Long-lasting and vivid, a tropical sculptural accent.
Plumeria Plumeria rubra Fragrant, velvety white and yellow blooms. Soft, lush texture for close-up placements.
