The Typography

Two faces, two registers — one that labels, one that breathes.

Cormorant SC

Small caps. The register that organizes.

Used for titles, section markers, eyebrows, filled-bar headers.

Cormorant Garamond

Body and italic. The register that breathes and grounds.

Used for all reading text, for significance lines, for the italic subtitles that carry emotional weight.

The Materials

What the day is built of — wood, linen, gold, hand-carved, hand-woven.

  • Gold flatware, no silver or chrome.
  • Linen tablecloths, natural weave.
  • Woven palm baskets, wooden serving structures.
  • Hand-carved details wherever possible.
  • No plastic, no synthetic — nothing that couldn't have been made a hundred years ago.
