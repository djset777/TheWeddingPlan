Traditions & Meaning

The why beneath every act.

The Ride

  • The bride arrives on horseback
  • Her father leads the horse the two-minute walk from Casa Valdez to Iglesia San Rafael
  • Guests watch from outside the chapel
  • Drone footage planned

The Memorial Chair

  • A single chair set apart for Monica, Julian's mother
  • Placed at Iglesia San Rafael only — the eight other angels are honored at the altar table at Casa Valdez
  • Framed photograph, small floral

The Lace Chapel Veils

  • Provided for women attending Iglesia San Rafael
  • Distributed at the chapel entrance
  • Guests may keep them as a memento

El Lazo

  • A figure-eight loop placed around the shoulders after the vows — the symbol of infinity
  • Padrinos de lazo · To be confirmed (separate from the godparents of rings and arras)
  • Form · To be confirmed (rosary · cord · floral)
  • Removed by the couple together at the end of the ceremony
  • Kept in the home after, framed and displayed

The Rings

  • Two yellow gold bands, in a small wooden box
  • Ring bearer · To be confirmed
  • Blessed at the altar during the ceremony
