The Brief

The Dress

Every detail is deliberate. Down to the hem.

The Vision

Allover lace, a corset bodice, moving sleeves, and a fitted-to-flared skirt: made for an open-air July afternoon in the Dominican mountains.

The Guide

Fabric

Must have

  • Allover lace
  • Light tulle or mesh lining

Worth exploring

  • Chantilly lace
  • Corded lace
  • Subtle sparkle lace
  • 3D floral appliqué

Skip

  • Satin
  • Mikado
  • Heavy rhinestones or crystals

Neckline

Must have

  • Open neckline

Worth exploring

  • Classic sweethearts
  • Inverted sweethearts
  • Scalloped strapless
  • Eyelash edges

Skip

  • High necklines
  • Mock necklines

Sleeves

Must have

  • Full-length lace coverage
  • Lace dense enough to soften a tattoo sleeve
  • A flare or opening along the arm

Worth exploring

  • Bell sleeves
  • Angel sleeves
  • Flared cuffs
  • Off, on, or higher on the shoulder

Skip

  • Detachable sleeves
  • Arm gauntlets
  • Sleeves snug from shoulder to wrist

Bodice

Must have

  • Structured corset, built in or worn over the dress
  • Support through the bust and midsection

Worth exploring

  • Underbust corsets
  • Victorian corsets
  • Basque waists
  • Dropped-V seams
  • Hidden, tonal, or exposed boning
  • Molded cups

Skip

  • Soft bodices with no corset structure

Silhouette

Must have

  • Defined waist
  • Room for the legs to move and breathe

Worth exploring

  • Trumpet
  • A-line
  • Fit and flare
  • Mermaid
  • Sheath
  • Draped skirts
  • Layered tulle skirts
  • High slits

Veil

Depends on the dress.
