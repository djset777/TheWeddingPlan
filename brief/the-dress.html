The Brief

The Dress

A reference for my girls — gowns worth a look.

The vision · an allover lace gown with a corset bodice, sleeves that open and move instead of hugging the arm, and a fitted-to-flared or A-line skirt, designed for an open-air July afternoon in the mountains of the Dominican Republic.

The Guide

Every category: what is required, what to try, what is ruled out.

Fabric

Breathable first, for an open-air July afternoon.

Required
Allover lace on a light tulle or mesh lining
To Try
Chantilly lace, corded lace, tulle skirt layers
Ruled Out
Satin, mikado, and other heavy fabrics that trap heat and damage easily

Corset

Structure that supports the bust and midsection.

Required
A corset
To Try
Built-in, overlay, or dropped-V seam; hidden, tonal, or exposed boning; molded, soft, or cupless support

Sleeves

Covered arms, with room to move.

Required
Full arm coverage in lace dense enough to soften a tattoo sleeve, opening or flaring somewhere along the arm
To Try
Off, on, or higher on the shoulder; a flare at the wrist, forearm, or elbow, or an angel sleeve
Ruled Out
Detachable sleeves, arm gauntlets, and sleeves snug from shoulder to wrist

Neckline

Open, and cool enough for July.

Required
An open neckline
To Try
Classic sweetheart, eyelash edge, inverted sweetheart, scalloped strapless
Ruled Out
High and mock necklines

Silhouette

Fitted to flared, tried every way.

To Try
Trumpet first, then A-line (especially under an overlay corset), then mermaid

Details

One of each, to be sure.

To Try
One bolero, one ballgown, one subtle sparkle lace, one 3D floral appliqué
Ruled Out
Heavy rhinestone or crystal

Veil

Still open.

To Try
To be decided once the dress direction is set
